The OrgAI mark: a lead and two reports joined by reporting lines.

- `mark.svg` is the full mark: the gradient from `brand-from` to `brand-to` on a `#0b0d12` rounded square. Use it at 32 px beside the wordmark and at 28 px alone in the collapsed sidebar. Keep clear space of half the mark's width.
- `mark-glyph.svg` is the glyph alone in a single ink. Its ink is `currentColor` in the app; shown as an image here it renders black, so use it only where the app can set the colour.
- The wordmark "OrgAI" is live text beside the mark, 16 px at weight 600 in `text-primary`. Never colour it with the accent or the gradient.
- Do not redraw, recolour, rotate or animate the mark.
