Acme · Onboarding review

Q3 findings

New customers take longest between signing and their first login. The interviews point to one cause: account setup needs an administrator, and most teams do not know who theirs is.

1. Where people get stuck

The welcome email arrives before the account exists, so the first link people click leads to an error page.

1

Administrators are asked for billing details they rarely have to hand, and the form cannot be saved part way through.

2. What we recommend

Send the welcome email only once the account is ready, and let administrators finish billing later.
